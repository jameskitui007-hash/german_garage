What We Need From You

For Diagnostics & Programming

Send us your vehicle's VIN (17-character Vehicle Identification Number), describe the symptoms or fault codes, and let us know your vehicle's model year and variant.

For Key Programming

Provide your VIN, specify if it's a lost key or spare key situation, and include proof of ownership. We'll give you a quote including key sourcing and programming.

For Parts Sourcing

Share your VIN and describe the parts needed. If possible, include photos or part numbers. We'll identify the exact components and provide pricing options.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find my VIN?

Your VIN is located on the driver's side dashboard (visible through windshield), driver's door jamb, vehicle registration, or insurance documents.

Do you service commercial trucks?

No, we exclusively service Mercedes and BMW passenger cars and vans. We do not work on commercial trucks or heavy vehicles.

Can you come to my location?

For some services, yes. Contact us to discuss mobile diagnostics or on-site programming availability in Nairobi.

How long does programming take?

Depends on the service. Key programming: 1-2 hours. ECU coding: 2-4 hours. Module replacement with online coding may take longer.
