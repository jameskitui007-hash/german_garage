Why VIN-Based Sourcing?

VIN decoding ensures the part matches your vehicle's exact build specifications, preventing fitment issues.

Avoid Wrong Parts

Mercedes and BMW have multiple variants per model year. The VIN identifies your specific configuration.

Faster Service

Professional Advice

Part Quality Options

Genuine Parts

Factory-original parts from Mercedes-Benz or BMW with full manufacturer warranty. Highest quality and guaranteed compatibility.

OEM Parts

Parts manufactured by the same suppliers that make genuine parts (Bosch, Continental, Hella, ZF, etc.) but without the manufacturer's logo. Identical quality at lower prices.

Quality Aftermarket

High-quality aftermarket parts from reputable brands for non-critical components. Cost-effective option with good reliability.
